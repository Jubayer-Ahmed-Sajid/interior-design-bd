# Nibas — Interior Design BD

A visual-first interior design platform for Bangladesh. See it. Price it. Trust it.

Most Dhaka interior firms hide their rates behind lead forms and show only 3D renders. Nibas does the opposite: completed flats with their **actual finished costs**, an **instant itemised budget estimator**, and **verified designer matching** under a written-contract standard.

## What's inside (v1)

- **Hero** — dark premium opening (charcoal, dark walnut, brass-gold) with a floating Instant Budget Estimate card
- **Completed Projects gallery** — filterable by room/style; every card shows neighbourhood, flat size, verified badge, and the real finished cost in taka (Bangladeshi lakh grouping, e.g. ৳28,50,000)
- **Before / After slider** — draggable comparison: bare concrete shell → finished interior
- **Budget Estimator** — flat size (400–3,000 sq ft) × finish tier, itemised BOQ, 5% VAT, total in a gold band
- **Verified Designers** — upfront per-sq-ft rates, projects completed, ratings and homeowner reviews
- **Quote request modal** — name + phone + neighbourhood, size prefilled from the estimator

## Estimator model

| Tier | Rate (per sq ft) |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | ৳1,200 |
| Standard | ৳2,200 |
| Premium | ৳4,200 |

Tier rates are midpoints of researched Dhaka market bands (budget ৳900–1,800, mid ৳1,500–3,000, premium ৳3,000–6,000 per sq ft). Itemised split: Structural & Civil Works 45% · Interior Works 27% · Lighting & Electrical 6% · Furniture & Fittings 17% · Design & Consultation 5% · plus 5% VAT.
